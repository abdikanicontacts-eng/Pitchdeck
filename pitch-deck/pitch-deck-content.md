## Slide 2 — The Problem

### Small Shops Still Struggle With Manual Business Management

Many small business owners rely on notebooks and manual calculations to manage their businesses.

### Key Challenges

- Poor inventory tracking
- Lost or inaccurate sales records
- Difficulty tracking customer debts
- Limited visibility into profits
- Time-consuming manual calculations

**Result:** Business owners can lose money without knowing where the problem is.

---

## Slide 3 — The Solution

### Meet DukaFlow

DukaFlow is a simple digital platform that helps small businesses manage their daily operations in one place.

### With DukaFlow, business owners can:

- Track inventory
- Record sales
- Manage customer debts
- Monitor profits
- Receive low-stock alerts
- View simple business reports

**One platform. Simple tools. Better decisions.**

---

## Slide 4 — How It Works

### From Daily Sales to Business Insights

**1. Add Products**  
Create your product list and stock quantities.

**2. Record Sales**  
Quickly record every transaction.

**3. Automatic Stock Updates**  
Inventory changes as products are sold.

**4. Track Debts**  
Record customer balances and payments.

**5. View Reports**  
See sales, expenses and estimated profit.

**6. Get Alerts**  
Know when important products are running low.
